### Step 3: Revenue Model Architecture

**TRANSACTIONAL MODELS**

- **Direct Sales**: One-time purchase

**PLATFORM MODELS**

- **Two-Sided**: Connecting buyers/sellers
- **Multi-Sided**: Multiple stakeholder groups
- **Ecosystem**: App stores, APIs
- **Aggregator**: Consolidating supply

**HYBRID MODELS**

- **Land & Expand**: Initial + growth revenue

### Step 5: Unit Economics Modeling

#### SaaS Unit Economics

```
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
├─ Marketing Spend / New Customers
├─ Sales Costs / New Customers
└─ Onboarding Costs / New Customers

LTV (Lifetime Value)
├─ ARPU × Gross Margin % × Customer Lifetime
├─ Consider: Expansion Revenue
└─ Consider: Referral Value

LTV:CAC Ratio Target: >3:1
Payback Period Target: <12 months
```

#### Marketplace Unit Economics

```
Take Rate: Commission %
├─ GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)
├─ Net Revenue = GMV × Take Rate
└─ Contribution Margin = Net Revenue - Variable Costs

Unit Economics per Transaction:
├─ Revenue per Transaction
├─ (-) Payment Processing
├─ (-) Customer Support
├─ (-) Delivery/Fulfillment
└─ = Contribution per Transaction
```

## Advanced Business Model Patterns

### Network Effects Design

```
DIRECT NETWORK EFFECTS
Users ↔ Users
(Social networks, messaging)

INDIRECT NETWORK EFFECTS
Supply ↔ Demand
(Marketplaces, platforms)

DATA NETWORK EFFECTS
Data → Better Product → More Users → More Data
(Recommendation engines, AI products)
```

### Moat Building Strategies

1. **Switching Costs**: Make it expensive to leave
2. **Network Effects**: Value increases with users
3. **Data Advantage**: Unique data access
4. **Brand**: Premium positioning
5. **Economies of Scale**: Unit cost advantages
6. **Regulatory**: Compliance as barrier

### Platform Business Design

**Core Components**:

- **Producers**: Create value
- **Consumers**: Consume value
- **Platform**: Facilitates exchange
- **Value Unit**: What's exchanged

**Monetization Layers**:

- Access fees (subscription)
- Transaction fees (commission)
- Enhanced access (premium features)
- Enhanced curation (promoted listings)

## Example Business Model Output

**Product**: AI-Powered Design Collaboration Platform

**Business Model Summary**:

```markdown
### Value Proposition

For distributed design teams who struggle with version control and feedback loops, our AI-powered platform provides real-time collaboration with intelligent suggestions, unlike traditional tools that require manual coordination.

### Revenue Model

- **Core**: Team subscriptions ($50/user/month)
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing + services
- **Marketplace**: 30% commission on templates

### Unit Economics (at scale)

- CAC: $500
- LTV: $2,400
- LTV:CAC: 4.8:1
- Payback: 10 months
- Gross Margin: 75%

### 3-Year Projection

- Year 1: $2M ARR (200 customers)
- Year 2: $8M ARR (800 customers)
- Year 3: $25M ARR (2,500 customers)
```

## Business Model Validation

### Key Metrics to Track

- **Product-Market Fit**: NPS > 50
- **Unit Economics**: LTV:CAC > 3:1
- **Growth**: MoM growth > 10%
- **Retention**: Monthly churn < 5%
- **Efficiency**: CAC payback < 12 months

### Risk Assessment

**Revenue Risks**:

- Customer concentration
- Churn rates
- Competition pricing pressure
- Market size limitations

**Cost Risks**:

- CAC inflation
- Infrastructure scaling
- Support costs
- Regulatory compliance
