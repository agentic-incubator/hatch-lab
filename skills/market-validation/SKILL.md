# Market Validation Skill

This skill helps you validate market opportunities, size addressable markets, and understand competitive dynamics. Using proven frameworks, we'll determine if your product idea has sufficient market potential.

## Core Capabilities

I can help you:

- Calculate TAM, SAM, and SOM for your opportunity
- Analyze competitive landscapes and positioning
- Identify and size customer segments
- Validate willingness to pay
- Assess market timing and trends
- Develop go-to-market strategies

## Validation Framework

### Step 1: Market Sizing

#### TAM (Total Addressable Market)

```text
TAM = [Total Potential Users] × [Annual Value per User]
```

Methods I'll guide you through:

1. **Top-Down**: Industry reports → Market subset
2. **Bottom-Up**: Customer count × Average price
3. **Value Theory**: Value created × Capturable percentage

#### SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

```text
SAM = TAM × [Geographic Reach %] × [Product Fit %]
```

Factors we'll consider:

- Geographic limitations
- Regulatory constraints
- Technical requirements
- Customer sophistication

#### SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

```text
SOM = SAM × [Realistic Market Share %]
```

Based on:

- Competition intensity
- Go-to-market resources
- Brand strength
- First-mover advantages

### Step 3: Competitive Analysis

#### Competition Mapping Matrix

```text
         Low Functionality    High Functionality
         ↑                    ↑
High    │ Overserved         │ Premium
Price   │ (Disruption        │ (Market leaders)
        │  opportunity)      │
        ├────────────────────┤
Low     │ Underserved        │ Value Players
Price   │ (Blue ocean)       │ (Volume focus)
        ↓                    ↓
```

#### Competitive Intelligence Framework

1. **Direct Competitors**: Same problem, same solution approach
2. **Indirect Competitors**: Same problem, different approach
3. **Substitute Solutions**: Different problem framing
4. **Do-Nothing Alternative**: Status quo

### Step 4: Willingness to Pay Validation

Methods we'll employ:

**Van Westendorp Price Sensitivity**

- Too expensive
- Too cheap
- Expensive but considerable
- Bargain price

**Conjoint Analysis Elements**

- Feature importance ranking
- Price-feature tradeoffs
- Optimal bundle identification

### Step 5: Market Dynamics Assessment

#### Trend Analysis

```text
MACRO TRENDS
    ↓

    ↓

```

#### Market Timing Evaluation

- **Too Early**: Market education needed
- **Right Time**: Demand + feasibility aligned
- **Too Late**: Entrenched competition

## Red Flags to Watch For

⚠️ **Market Too Small**: SOM < $10M
⚠️ **Overcrowded**: >20 direct competitors
⚠️ **No Clear Differentiation**: Competing on price alone
⚠️ **Poor Unit Economics**: CAC > 3x LTV
⚠️ **Bad Timing**: Market declining or not ready
