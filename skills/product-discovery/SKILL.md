# Product Discovery Skill

This skill helps you systematically explore problem spaces and identify high-value opportunities for innovation. It combines
established product discovery methodologies to uncover unmet needs and validate problem-solution fit.

## Core Capabilities

I can help you:

- Map customer journeys and identify pain points
- Apply jobs-to-be-done framework for deep customer understanding
- Frame problems using the double diamond approach
- Generate and prioritize opportunity areas
- Develop initial product hypotheses

## Discovery Process

### Step 1: Problem Space Definition

I'll guide you through defining your problem space by asking:

1. **Target Domain**: What industry or domain are you exploring?
2. **Initial Hypothesis**: What problem do you believe exists?
3. **Stakeholders**: Who experiences this problem?
4. **Current State**: How is this problem currently addressed?

### Step 2: Customer Journey Mapping

For your identified stakeholders, we'll map:

```
BEFORE → DURING → AFTER
│         │         │
├─ Triggers      ├─ Actions       ├─ Outcomes
├─ Context       ├─ Pain Points   ├─ Emotions
├─ Expectations  ├─ Workarounds   ├─ Next Steps
└─ Alternatives  └─ Failures      └─ Consequences
```

### Step 3: Jobs-to-Be-Done Analysis

We'll decompose customer needs into:

**Functional Jobs**

- Primary tasks to accomplish
- Measurable outcomes desired
- Success criteria

**Emotional Jobs**

- Feelings to achieve or avoid
- Social perceptions
- Personal identity aspects

**Social Jobs**

- Status signaling
- Group belonging
- Relationship management

### Step 4: Problem Framing

Using insights gathered, we'll frame problems as:

```
[STAKEHOLDER] needs a way to [JOB-TO-BE-DONE]
because [INSIGHT/REASON]
but [CURRENT BARRIER/LIMITATION]
which leads to [NEGATIVE OUTCOME]
```

### Step 5: Opportunity Scoring

We'll evaluate opportunities using:

| Criterion               | Weight | Score (1-10) |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ------------ |
| Problem Severity        | 25%    | \_\_\_       |
| Frequency of Occurrence | 20%    | \_\_\_       |
| Market Size             | 20%    | \_\_\_       |
| Solution Feasibility    | 15%    | \_\_\_       |
| Competitive Advantage   | 10%    | \_\_\_       |
| Strategic Alignment     | 10%    | \_\_\_       |

## Example Discovery Session

**Domain**: Corporate training

**Problem Discovery Output**:

- **Problem**: Mid-level managers struggle to develop leadership skills
- **Job-to-be-Done**: "Help me become a confident leader without sacrificing my technical work"
- **Key Insight**: Traditional training requires too much time away from core responsibilities
- **Opportunity**: Micro-learning platform with AI-powered coaching
- **Validation Metrics**:
  - Time to skill acquisition
  - Manager confidence scores
  - Team performance improvements

## Best Practices

1. **Start Broad, Then Narrow**: Explore the entire problem space before focusing
2. **Talk to Real Users**: Validate assumptions with actual stakeholders
3. **Document Everything**: Capture all insights for future reference
4. **Iterate Rapidly**: Test and refine problem definitions
5. **Avoid Solution Bias**: Focus on problems before jumping to solutions

## Questions I'll Ask You

To maximize our discovery session, be prepared to discuss:

- Who are your target users/customers?
- What triggered your interest in this problem space?
- What existing solutions have you observed?
- What unique insights or access do you have?
- What constraints (technical, regulatory, resource) exist?
